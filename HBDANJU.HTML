CHAPTER ONE — THE STORYTELLER

Some people collect years.
You collect stories.

Every year you find new words for feelings the rest of us can't quite name. You notice the light before anyone else does, the pause before someone speaks, the sentence hiding inside an ordinary Tuesday. That's not a small gift — it's the reason the people around you feel a little more seen.

Today isn't about another candle. It's about the whole library you've written since the last one — every page, every draft, every quiet 2 a.m. idea you were brave enough to keep.

CHAPTER THREE — WHAT I WISH FOR YOU

Not resolutions. Just wishes.

  • A blank page that never feels intimidating — only full of possibility.
  • Readers, real and imagined, who understand exactly what you meant.
  • Days generous enough to give you both the words and the rest.
  • A year written entirely in your own voice, no edits needed.
